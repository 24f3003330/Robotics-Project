Real-time · Multimodal · Robot-ready

Real-time multimodal emotion recognition

Recognise human emotion live from a face on camera or from the tone of a voice on the microphone, or analyse handwriting written on screen for signs of anxiety, depression and stress, or classify body vitals (ECG, skin conductance, respiration) into WESAD's baseline, stress and amusement conditions. Each detected camera or voice emotion can drive mood music and an xArm robot that hands over a matching bottle.

🎥 Camera Emotion Recognition 🎙️ Voice Emotion Recognition ✍️ Handwriting Analysis 💓 Body Vitals
🎥

Camera / Facial Emotion Recognition

Streams your webcam, detects faces with OpenCV and reads their expression frame by frame. The detected emotion is stabilised over several frames before it is used.

  • Live webcam feed with face overlay
  • Continuous face detection and emotion updates
  • Uses the camera only - never the microphone

🎙️

Voice / Speech Emotion Recognition

Listens to the microphone continuously, finds speech with voice-activity detection and classifies every 2-4 s of speech with a HuBERT model. No speech-to-text: only the tone of voice matters, never the words.

  • Happy, angry, sad and neutral probabilities, live
  • Predictions keep updating until you stop listening
  • Separate models: speaker gender and an estimated age group (approximate, self-reported training labels)
  • Uses the microphone only - never the camera
Open voice recognition →
✍️

Handwriting: Anxiety, Depression, Stress

Write a word or sentence on a canvas with a mouse, trackpad, touchscreen or stylus. The pen trajectory (timing, speed, strokes, pressure and tilt where available) is analysed by a small attention-based Transformer trained on the EMOTHAW dataset.

  • Anxiety, Depression and Stress: each normal or elevated, with its probability
  • Separate prototype: Happy, Neutral, Sad or Angry, trained on graphology-based seed data (not validated)
  • Analyses on demand when you press Analyze
  • No camera and no microphone
Open handwriting recognition →
💓

Body Vitals: Baseline, Stress, Amusement

Heart rate and HRV from ECG, skin conductance (GSR/EDA) and respiration are turned into physiological features, compared with the person's own 5-minute resting reference and classified by an SVM trained on the WESAD dataset, evaluated on held-out subjects.

  • WESAD's own conditions: Baseline, Stress or Amusement, with probabilities
  • 29 physiological features (HR, HRV, EDA, respiration) → one trained model
  • Demo mode with clearly labelled simulated inputs until sensors are connected
  • No camera and no microphone
