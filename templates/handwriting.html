About this model

-

A small Transformer reads the pen trajectory (position, timing, speed, acceleration, direction, stroke boundaries and, from a stylus, pressure and pen altitude) and pools it with learned attention. It is trained on the EMOTHAW dataset and predicts that dataset's three targets independently: Anxiety, Depression and Stress, each normal or elevated (above the normal band of the participant's DASS-42 questionnaire score). These are not the camera or voice emotions.

This is a research demonstration, not a diagnosis. EMOTHAW was recorded with a Wacom pen tablet; mouse, trackpad or finger writing moves differently and reports no pressure or tilt, which lowers reliability.

Happy · Neutral · Sad · Angry is a separate, prototype model with its own checkpoint. No suitable public handwriting dataset with these four emotions is available yet, so it is trained on seed data derived from graphology reference examples. Graphology is not a scientifically validated source of emotion labels: the seed data only exists to build and test the 4-class pipeline and will be replaced by a real emotion-labelled dataset. Where the examples were images, the stroke order was estimated and no timing or pressure exists, so this model uses the shape of the writing only.

You can write on the canvas or upload a handwriting image (PNG, JPG or WEBP). An uploaded image is turned into estimated strokes; it has no timing or pen pressure.

Samples collected here are saved as raw canvas trajectories (positions, timing and, from a stylus, pressure and tilt) with the anonymous writer ID and the label the participant chose. They are used to train the 4-class model with a writer-independent evaluation. The labels are the participants' choices, not validated emotional states.
